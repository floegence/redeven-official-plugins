Redeven Official Mind Map Plugin - Third-Party Notices

The application icon, document model, layout algorithm and Canvas renderer were
created for this plugin. No third-party audio or font assets are included.

The application bundles released Floe Webapp Core, ReDevPlugin UI and Contracts,
Solid, Noble Hashes, clsx and tailwind-merge code. The complete upstream license
texts are generated from the exact installed packages in BUNDLED-LICENSES.txt.
Interface icons are provided by the released Floe icon components.

The WASM worker uses redevplugin-worker-sdk, base64, serde and serde_json.
BUNDLED-LICENSES.txt also includes the license texts from every exact published
crate in the locked Worker dependency graph, including build dependencies.
