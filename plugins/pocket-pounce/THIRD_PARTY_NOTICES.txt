Redeven Official Pocket Pounce Plugin - Third-Party Notices

Pocket Pounce does not contain third-party visual, audio, or font assets. The
jerboa character, desert scene, icon, animation, gameplay implementation, and
original software 3D renderer were created for this plugin from original
geometry and code.

Interaction and camera research consulted the MIT-licensed Night's Watch Games
Jump Jump project. Pocket Pounce adapts the general design principles of a high
oblique camera, charge compression, landing recovery, and post-landing camera
follow to its own forward-only physics, original character, and Canvas2D
renderer. No upstream source or assets are copied.
https://github.com/NightsWatchGames/jump-jump

Renderer research also consulted the MIT-licensed Zdog scene-graph approach.
No Zdog source or assets are included.
https://github.com/metafizzy/zdog

Projects without an explicit repository license were excluded; their source
and assets were not used.

JavaScript build dependencies retain their respective upstream licences. The
released package contains compiled output only.
